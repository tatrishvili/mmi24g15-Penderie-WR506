### Proposed design to develop next (not implemented)

1. **Separate child authentication from adult authentication.** A child must receive
   a restricted security principal/session, never the parent's token with a hidden
   UI. No child email is required for the initial version. Use hashed long passwords,
   account-aware + IP throttling, generic login failures, session rotation and parent
   recovery/revocation. Temporary credentials must expire and not be logged or put
   in notifications. Do not turn the existing 4–6 digit PIN into a remote password.
2. **Explicit guardianship.** Tie each child to responsible guardian account(s).
   Creating the child establishes the creator as guardian. Another household adult
   is not automatically a guardian just because they are an adult. Design invitation/
   consent for adding a guardian instead of silently granting it. Confirm the small
   remaining policy choices only when necessary; do not repeat decided questions.
3. **Item owner != location.** Every item retains its owner profile independent of
   its room or storage. Example: a parent's coat and a child's coat can both be in
   the common hallway; the child still sees/manages only its own coat.
4. **Room access.** Household-owned rooms should be assignable to one or multiple
   profiles, or marked common. Children can choose assigned/granted/common rooms
   for their own items. Siblings may share a bedroom without sharing inventory.
   Storage inherits room restrictions and must not expand access. A guardian can
   correct assignments. Do not reveal an adult's private room/path through a child
   item, list, search, photo, QR endpoint or external share. Handle existing items
   carefully if a room's access changes; do not orphan inventory or silently delete it.
5. **Child collections.** Persist ownership by the child profile; the current adult
   SocialWorkspace/CollectionPublication model is adult-owner based, so simply
   exposing those existing endpoints to children is unsafe. Private by default.
   Guardians can manage their own children's resources; explain to children that
   "private" hides from friends, not from their responsible guardian.
6. **Friendships.** Parent-managed child identities must participate as child
   identities, not fake verified adult Users. Approve outgoing/incoming child
   friendships; specify reciprocal child/guardian acceptance. Explicitly approve
   named adult relatives as family. No global child directory, anonymous links,
   app-wide child publication, stranger contacts, selling/lending or DMs in this slice.
7. **Approval workflow.** Child chooses approved recipient(s) and submits a request.
   Freeze the reviewed names/photos/fields and recipient set/version. Parent sees
   the exact preview in **Notifications** and can Accept/Decline. The server rechecks
   guardian authority, recipients, ownership, source vetoes and versions inside the
   approval transaction. Changes to content/recipients after review must invalidate
   the request or require a fresh approval; no permanent blanket permission to
   publish later edits. New friends must not silently receive old approvals.
8. **Notifications.** Durable inbox with unread counts, request details, Accept/
   Decline and a child-facing decision notification. A notification references the
   authoritative approval request; reading a notification is not approval. Prevent
   cross-family access, duplicate approval races, self-approval by the child and
   approvals after guardian/access removal. Audit decisions; allow revocation.
9. **Revocation.** Guardian can disable access/reset credentials/revoke sessions and
   external shares. Child should always be able to stop a share; making a resource
   less visible must not require waiting for approval. Removed friendship, archived/
   deleted/Personal source content and withdrawn approval must block future photo
   and data requests, including the feed.
10. **Delivery order.** First establish restricted actors and endpoint authorization;
    then child setup/login/recovery; room/storage access; child-owned collections;
    approved connections; approval inbox and safe publication; end-to-end tests.
    Update PRODUCT_SPEC, WORKFLOWS and PERMISSIONS with the final agreed behavior.

### Security references consulted

- NIST current authentication guidance:
  https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html
  Password-only recommendation: minimum 15 characters, allow long passphrases and
  password managers, reject common/compromised passwords, avoid arbitrary periodic
  rotation/security questions. A local device PIN activating a cryptographic key is
  different from a tiny password checked by an internet server.
- CNIL guidance on minors' digital rights and privacy-respecting parental controls:
  https://www.cnil.fr/fr/enjeux-numeriques/les-droits-numeriques-des-mineurs
  Explain guardian access clearly and age-appropriately. Do not claim that an app
  policy of age 12+, parental creation or a birth-date field proves age/guardianship
  or automatically settles launch-jurisdiction requirements.
